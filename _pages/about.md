I currently serve as a Senior Principal AI Researcher and Research Lead at `Microsoft Gaming (King)`. I earned my Ph.D. in Robotics and Artificial Intelligence from [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/) in collaboration with [The University of Melbourne](https://www.unimelb.edu.au/) in 2016.

With 16+ years of industry experience in `Private Equity`, `Gaming`, `Telecom`, `Geoinformatics`, and `E-commerce`, I have published in premier conferences such as NeurIPS, ICLR, CVPR, AAAI, EMNLP, IJCAI, AISTATS and KDD, and regularly serve as a reviewer for these venues. I have supervised 15 Master’s and 2 Ph.D. students.

My research focuses on representation learning on time series and graphs, multimodal language model evaluation, agentic systems, GenAI content detection, and AI4Science. If you are interested in "*transforming industries through AI research*". I generally welcome research collaboration inquiries at `lele@cspaper.org`.
