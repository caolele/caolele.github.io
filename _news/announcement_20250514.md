**"They think they'll spend 90% of their time building models. They won't."** — Yes, I said that. And no, I didn't make up the number... or maybe I did. You know what they say: "Over 87% of all statistics are made up on the spot."

With a background that zigzags through software engineering, design, and AI/ML, and industry experience across telecom, e-commerce, clinical medicine, geoinformatics, private equity, and now gaming at King (a part of Microsoft) — I've seen AI from nearly every angle.

We cover a smorgasbord of today's favorite buzzwords:
- GenAI
- Document Layout Analysis
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
- Graphs
- LLMs & Agents
- AI Research

If you're in AI, hiring for AI, or just commuting to your AI work — this one's for you.
