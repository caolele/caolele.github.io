I'm excited to announce the release of my new book: **A Practical Guide to Detect GenAI Content**!

- [Kindle Edition](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0F2SXZ7DJ)
- [Paperback Edition](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0F2ZKH2R4)

After joining a [Nobel Prize Roundtable on AI Content Governance](https://llcresearch.com/news/announcement_20240306/) in March 2024, I couldn't stop thinking: how do we, as practitioners, stay grounded in truth in the GenAI era?

Many late nights of reading, surveying, and writing later — I'm happy to share the first edition of this book with you.

Today, we're seeing Studio Ghibli-style images and videos flooding the internet, reigniting debate around AI-generated content governance. This makes the need for practical, cross-modal GenAI discerning more urgent than ever. The book breaks it down across three core chapters:

- **Text**: Model-based detection, watermarking, linguistic cues
- **Visual**: Deepfake spotting, reverse image tracing, detection tools
- **Audio**: Voice cloning & AI music analysis, tools like AudioSeal

Distilled from 300+ research papers into an approachable, hands-on guide, built for cybersecurity pros, educators, journalists, and curious digital citizens alike. No PhD required — just curiosity and purpose.

This field is evolving fast — I plan to work on a 2nd Edition. If you're passionate about AI integrity and truth in media, let's connect!
