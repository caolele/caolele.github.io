## Trust, autonomy, and the evolving role of developers

The panel exchanged perspectives on **trust**, **autonomy**, and the evolving role of developers as agentic systems mature — where AI assistance ends and AI delegation begins, and what that shift means for the people building software.

## From software engineering to system stewardship

One idea I shared on stage:

<div class="row mt-3 align-items-center">
    <div class="col-md-5 mb-3 mb-md-0">
        <img src="/assets/img/news/20260428-3.jpeg" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" alt="Lele Cao speaking on stage at AgenticDevDays 2026">
    </div>
    <div class="col-md-7">
        <blockquote>
            The future "developer" may not primarily write code, but design, supervise, and evolve self-improving agents that continuously generate, test, critique, and refine software systems. We are moving from software engineering toward <strong>system stewardship</strong> — defining goals, constraints, feedback loops, and trust boundaries for autonomous builders. The most important skill may soon be not coding faster, but orchestrating intelligence responsibly.
        </blockquote>
